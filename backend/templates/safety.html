🛡️ Mobile Money Safety Tips

Simple steps to help protect yourself from Mobile Money scams.

1. Pause before sending money

Don't rush because someone is putting pressure on you. Take a moment to verify the situation.

2. Don't automatically refund a "wrong" payment

Someone may claim that they accidentally sent money to your number. Don't send money back simply because they say so.

3. Check your actual balance

Don't rely only on what someone tells you or on a screenshot. Check your own Mobile Money balance or transaction history.

4. Never share your PIN

Never give your Mobile Money PIN to another person.

5. Be careful with links

Don't click suspicious links sent through SMS, WhatsApp, social media, or email.

6. Don't share OTPs

Never share a one-time password or verification code with someone who calls or messages you unexpectedly.

7. Verify before trusting

If someone claims to represent a company, bank, or Mobile Money service, verify through an official channel.

🚨 Remember

Pause. Verify. Protect your money.
